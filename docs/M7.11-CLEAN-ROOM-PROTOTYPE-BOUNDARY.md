# M7.11 — Clean-room independent-model boundary

Jend distinguishes provider-backed third-party models, open/licensed models, and
Jend-independent models.

Internal/private experimentation is not treated as permission to reverse
engineer or distill a restricted provider. A Jend-independent model may use
reviewed open/licensed weights or datasets, human-authored data, Jend-owned
synthetic data, public benchmarks, and public specifications.

Restricted provider outputs, unknown/leaked checkpoints, unreviewed rights and
provider impersonation fail closed.

Jend may independently target broad capabilities such as reasoning, coding,
long context, tool use, multimodal understanding and writing. Public benchmarks
and Jend-owned evaluation suites should measure those goals instead of using a
closed provider as a hidden training oracle.

A real third-party model remains `ProviderBacked`; a Jend prototype remains
`JendIndependent` and uses a Jend-owned product identity.
